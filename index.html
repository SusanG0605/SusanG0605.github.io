About Me

Hi, I am Susan Guan. I am a senior studio art major student from China. I like to make different kinds of artworks but mostly focus on illustration. I love combining traditional art with digital painting.

I worked with Bible Visual International for a year and a half, and I am currently an illustrator intern at Bob Jones Press.

My work focuses on creativity, storytelling, and visual expression. I explore different mediums and ideas to express my thoughts and emotions.

This portfolio showcases my selected works and personal projects.
